Security

The security of student data is a top priority for InsightScholar. This page outlines the measures we take to keep your data safe.

Our Approach to Security

InsightScholar was designed with security in mind from day one. We follow industry best practices to protect your school's data and ensure that only authorised users can access it.

Technical Security Measures

  • HTTPS encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS.
  • Password hashing: Passwords are never stored in plain text. We use bcrypt hashing, a strong one-way cryptographic algorithm.
  • Session management: Sessions are cryptographically signed and expire automatically after inactivity.
  • Input validation: All user-submitted data is validated and sanitised to prevent injection attacks.
  • CSRF protection: All forms are protected against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks.

Access Control

InsightScholar enforces strict role-based access control:

  • Super Admin: Platform-level access. Can manage schools but cannot access individual student records.
  • School Admin: Full access to their school's data only. Cannot access data from other schools.
  • Teacher: Can manage students and generate reports within their school.
  • Student: Can only view their own published report cards.
  • Parent: Can only view published reports for their linked children.

Every database query is scoped to the logged-in user's school. It is not possible for one school to access another school's data.

Data Protection

  • Student data is stored only for the duration of the school's active subscription.
  • Deleted data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered.
  • We do not sell, share, or use student data for any purpose other than providing the service.

Your Role in Security

You can help keep your school's data secure by following these best practices:

  • Use a strong, unique password for your InsightScholar account.
  • Never share your login credentials with anyone.
  • Log out when using a shared or public computer.
  • Contact your school administrator immediately if you suspect your account has been compromised.
  • School administrators should promptly disable accounts for staff who are no longer employed.

Incident Response

In the event of a data breach or security incident, we will:

  • Investigate and contain the incident as quickly as possible
  • Notify affected schools within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach
  • Provide details of what data was affected and what steps we are taking
  • Work to prevent recurrence

Report a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in InsightScholar, please report it responsibly. We take all security reports seriously and will respond promptly.

Please do not publicly disclose the vulnerability until we have had a reasonable opportunity to address it.
