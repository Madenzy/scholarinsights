Cookie Policy

InsightScholar uses only essential cookies required for the platform to function. We do not use tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember information about your visit, such as your login session, which makes the site easier to use.

Cookies We Use

InsightScholar uses a minimal number of cookies, all of which are strictly necessary for the platform to function:

Session Cookie

  • Name: session
  • Purpose: Keeps you logged in while you use the platform. Without this cookie, you would be logged out every time you navigate to a new page.
  • Duration: Deleted when you close your browser (unless you select "Keep me signed in", in which case it persists for up to 30 days).
  • Type: Essential — cannot be disabled without breaking core functionality.

Remember Me Cookie

  • Name: remember_token
  • Purpose: Only set if you check "Keep me signed in" on the login page. Allows you to stay logged in across browser sessions.
  • Duration: 30 days.
  • Type: Functional — only set with your explicit consent.

Third-Party Cookies

InsightScholar loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may set their own cookies. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details. We do not use any advertising, analytics, or social media tracking cookies.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored and delete them individually
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies (note: this will prevent you from logging in to InsightScholar)
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Disabling essential cookies will prevent you from using InsightScholar, as we rely on them to keep you securely logged in.
